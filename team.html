Chairman

Mr. N. Chandrasekaran

Mr. N. Chandrasekaran, founder of CK Airtech, is a mechanical engineering graduate with over 40 years of experience in design and product development. Mr. Chandrasekaran brings strategic vision, deep expertise and a steadfast commitment to excellence.

Managing Director

Mr. R. Sakthi Mohan

Entrepreneurial Leader

Mr. R. Sakthi Mohan, a mechanical engineer with extensive experience in marketing and project management, embarked on his entrepreneurial journey after returning from overseas assignments. Inspired by Mr. Chandrasekaran, he ventured into air pollution control, focusing on securing small and medium-scale industries. As Managing Director, he continues to lead CK Airtech with passion and vision, driving innovation and growth alongside a team of skilled professionals.

Executive Director

Mr. V. Saravanan

With over 25 years of experience in the manufacturing sector, Mr. V. Saravanan is an expert in managing custom-built equipment, purchasing, and project management. Since joining CK Airtech in 1999, he has played a pivotal role in the company’s growth. As Executive Director, he oversees the execution of projects from design to commissioning, ensuring a team of over 150 professionals at the Hosur facility. His dedication ensures cost-effectiveness, timely project delivery and operational success.

Chief Growth Officer

Mr. Rajadurai Swaminathan

Strategy today. Success tomorrow.

With a strong background in business strategy, engineering and project management, Mr. Swaminathan leads our growth initiatives, channel development and strategic partnerships, helping us create long-term value and market leadership.
